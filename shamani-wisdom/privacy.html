Privacy Policy

Shamani Wisdom · Last updated 26 October 2026

This policy covers the Shamani Wisdom app. Shamani Wisdom uses Decypher Labs Studio ("the Studio"), an internal tool operated by Decypher Labs. It is used only by Decypher Labs to publish videos to, and read statistics from, YouTube channels and Instagram professional accounts that Decypher Labs owns or manages. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When an account owner connects an account, the Studio receives, through the official YouTube Data API and Instagram API:

  • the account's identifier and username;
  • an access token that lets the Studio publish videos and read insights for that account;
  • the account's own posts and their statistics (for example views, reach and interactions).

The Studio does not access the personal information of other people, does not read private messages, and does not collect information about the people who watch or follow the accounts beyond the aggregated statistics the platforms provide.

How we use it

Only to publish videos chosen by Decypher Labs to its own accounts, to schedule them, and to show the accounts' own performance statistics inside the Studio.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on Decypher Labs' own computer and are never published. Statistics are cached temporarily to show reports. Information is kept only while the account stays connected.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone. It is used only within the Studio.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting an account in the Studio deletes its stored token and cached statistics. You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Instagram or Google account settings, or ask us to delete everything as described on the Data Deletion Instructions page.
