Data Deletion Instructions

The Hidden Why · Decypher Labs Studio

The Studio stores only an encrypted access token and cached statistics for accounts that were connected to it. To delete them:

  1. Remove the app's access yourself: on Instagram, go to Settings → Security → Apps and websites, find the app and remove it. For YouTube, open your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access and remove it. The stored token stops working immediately.
  2. Ask us to delete everything: email decypherlabs.io@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the account's username. We delete the stored token and all cached statistics for that account within 7 days and confirm by email.
